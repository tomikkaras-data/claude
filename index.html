Crater Clash

Turn-based artillery battle for 2–8 players

#NameControlled byTeam

How to play
  • Players take turns. Each turn one character moves and attacks once before the timer runs out.
  • After attacking you get a few seconds to retreat.
  • Characters on the same team can't hurt each other. Water is deadly for everyone.
  • The last team with characters standing wins.
  • ←→ walk · ↑↓ aim · Enter jump · Backspace backflip
  • Space hold to charge, release to fire · 1–8 or Tab pick weapon
  • Air Strike and Teleport: click on the map. Esc pause · M mute
  • Wind pushes the bazooka and air strike missiles, but not grenades.

Crater Clash

Turn-based artillery battle for 2–8 players

How to play
  • Players take turns. Each turn one character moves and attacks once before the timer runs out.
  • After attacking you get a few seconds to retreat.
  • With friendly fire off, characters on the same team can't hurt each other. Turn it on and anyone can be hurt, including yourself.
  • Water is deadly for everyone, and falling from a height hurts.
  • The last team with characters standing wins.
  • ←→ walk · ↑↓ aim · Enter jump · Backspace backflip
  • Space hold to charge, release to fire · 1–9, 0, T, K or Tab pick weapon
  • Air Strike and Teleport: click on the map. Esc pause · M mute
  • Drag the map to look around, mouse wheel or +/− to zoom, C to re-centre.
  • Ninja Rope: shoot it at the ceiling or a wall, swing with ←→, climb with ↑↓, let go with Space or Enter. It doesn't use up your attack.
  • Grenades: press F (or the ⏱ button) to set the timer from 1 to 5 seconds.
  • Shotgun: two shots per turn. You can move between them.
  • Baseball Bat (B): swing at someone right next to you to send them flying. Flamethrower (G): sprays fire for a second and a half.
  • Crates: walk into one to open it. Wooden crates hold health, power-ups, armor or ammo. Steel crates hold special weapons: Gatling Gun, Sniper Rifle, Desert Eagle, Nuka Bomb, Robot Frog (splits into 5 baby frogs when it blows), Napalm Strike, Anvil Drop and Concrete Block (R rotates it).
  • Drill and Pickaxe: hold Space to dig down or in the aim direction. Like the rope, they don't use up your attack.
  • Mines go off shortly after an enemy gets close. Mines placed on the map at the start hurt everyone.
  • When only CPU players are left, press Skip to end to see who wins.
  • Touch screens: use the on-screen buttons, drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap the map for Air Strike and Teleport.
  • Wind pushes the bazooka and air strike missiles, but not grenades.

Weapon settings

Change how hard each weapon hits, how big a hole it blows, and how many each team starts with. Leave ammo empty for unlimited. Weapons marked crate are normally only found in steel crates.
